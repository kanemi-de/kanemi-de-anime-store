## 🌸 Welcome to KANEMI-DE

Welcome to **KANEMI-DE**, an online destination dedicated to anime figures, Japanese pop culture, collectible merchandise, and the passion of anime fans around the world.

Our mission is to create an exceptional shopping experience where collectors and anime enthusiasts can discover beautiful figures inspired by iconic characters, legendary battles, and unforgettable stories.

From legendary Dragon Ball warriors to the adventurous pirates of One Piece, KANEMI-DE aims to bring your favorite anime universes together in one elegant digital storefront.

**More than a store — a universe for collectors.**

At KANEMI-DE, we believe every anime figure represents a story, a memory, and a character worth celebrating.
